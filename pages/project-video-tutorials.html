Video Documentation · Product Workflows

From Written Instructions to Visual Walkthroughs

I scripted, recorded, and edited product tutorials to give users a visual way to learn workflows alongside written documentation.

Video Documentation · Product Workflows
CompanyKovai.co; Capgemini engagements
RoleTechnical Writer
PeriodAcross Kovai.co (2021–2023) and Capgemini (2024–present)
ProductDocument360 · Operations Management · Field Apps

The Problem

Users Needed a Visual Way to Follow Workflows

Written guidance was complemented by a need for visual walkthroughs of product tasks. The tutorials gave users another way to learn Document360, Operations Management, and Field Apps.

My Role

My Responsibilities

I worked as a Technical Writer, with responsibility for:

  • Script instructional tutorials
  • Record product workflows
  • Edit tutorial videos using Camtasia and Snagit
  • Integrate tutorials with the documentation experience
  • Create videos for Honeywell Operations Management and GE Vernova Field Apps

Process

How I Approached It

01

Focus on the Workflow

I developed tutorials around product tasks users needed to follow.

02

Write the Script

I scripted the walkthrough to explain the steps clearly before recording.

03

Capture the Product

I recorded workflows and prepared visual material using Camtasia and Snagit.

04

Edit the Tutorial

I edited the recordings into instructional videos that guided users through the task.

05

Connect with Written Help

I integrated videos into the documentation experience. For Operations Management and Field Apps, they complemented documentation I created from scratch.
