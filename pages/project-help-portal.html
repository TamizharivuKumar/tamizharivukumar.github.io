Projects/Online Help Portal Revamp

Online Help · Honeywell

Clearer Online Help. Fewer Support Tickets.

I restructured and rewrote online help content for Honeywell, combining clearer product guidance with improvements to content organisation and browser rendering.

30%
Fewer customer support tickets within two quarters
CompanyCapgemini, supporting Honeywell
RoleTechnical Writer
PeriodDuring Capgemini tenure: 2024–present
ProductOperations Management

The Problem

Help Content Needed a Clearer Structure

The help portal needed clearer organisation, more usable instructions, and reliable HTML/CSS presentation across browsers. For Operations Management, I also needed to establish documentation from scratch.

My Role

My Responsibilities

I worked as a Technical Writer, with responsibility for:

  • Restructure and rewrite online help content
  • Create the initial Operations Management documentation
  • Fix HTML/CSS rendering issues across browsers
  • Maintain version-controlled documentation updates
  • Evaluate outcomes using analytics and user feedback

Process

How I Approached It

01

Structure the Content

I organised the help content around product areas and user tasks, establishing an initial structure for Operations Management.

02

Rewrite the Instructions

I rewrote product guidance as clearer task-based instructions and used Confluence and MadCap Flare to author and maintain it.

03

Improve the Presentation

I corrected HTML/CSS rendering issues so that the help content displayed more reliably across browsers.

04

Maintain the Documentation

I used version control to support documentation updates and ongoing maintenance.

05

Assess the Outcome

I used internal analytics to track support-ticket reduction, with customer feedback and input from the L&D team supporting evaluation. Google Analytics helped assess portal usage.
