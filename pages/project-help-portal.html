Online Help Portal Revamp — 30% Support Ticket Reduction

I restructured and rewrote online help content for Honeywell, combining clearer product guidance with improvements to content organisation and browser rendering.

30%
Fewer customer support tickets within two quarters
OrganisationCapgemini, supporting Honeywell
RoleTechnical Writer
ProductOperations Management
PeriodDuring Capgemini tenure: 2024–present

01

Overview

As a Capgemini contractor supporting Honeywell, I improved online help to make product guidance easier to find and follow. Alongside the revamp, I created Operations Management documentation from scratch, establishing its content structure and initial user guidance.

02

My Contribution

My work covered information architecture, clearer task-based instructions, HTML/CSS rendering fixes across browsers, and version-controlled documentation updates. I used Confluence and MadCap Flare to author and maintain the content.

03

Results

Customer support tickets decreased by 30% within two quarters. Internal analytics tracked the reduction, while customer feedback and input from the Learning and Development team helped assess documentation effectiveness. Google Analytics also supported evaluation of portal usage.

04

Takeaway

Clear instructions, organised content, and reliable presentation work together to strengthen self-service support.
