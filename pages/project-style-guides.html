Content Standards · Honeywell

A Shared Standard for Writing and Visual Content

I developed practical authoring and image guidelines that adapted established technical writing standards to the product’s requirements.

Content Standards · Honeywell
CompanyCapgemini, supporting Honeywell
RoleTechnical Writer
PeriodDuring Capgemini tenure: 2024–present
ProductHoneywell product documentation

The Problem

General Standards Needed Product-Specific Guidance

The writing team needed practical guidance for consistent content and visual presentation. Established technical writing standards provided a baseline, but needed adaptation to the product’s requirements.

My Role

My Responsibilities

I created internal authoring and image style guides to establish consistent documentation practices across the technical writing team. I used the Microsoft Manual of Style for Technical Publications (MSTP) as the baseline and adapted its guidance to product requirements.

  • Create an internal authoring guide
  • Create image and screenshot guidance
  • Adapt MSTP principles to product needs
  • Define heading, accordion, image, and link conventions
  • Provide a shared reference for authoring and review

Process

How I Approached It

01

Choose the Baseline

I used the Microsoft Manual of Style for Technical Publications (MSTP) as the foundation.

02

Adapt to the Product

I translated general principles into guidance that suited the product’s documentation and presentation needs.

03

Define Content Conventions

I covered writing tone, formatting, headings, accordions, and links.

04

Define Visual Conventions

I established guidance for images and screenshot annotations to support consistent visual content.

05

Support Authoring and Review

I brought the guidance together as a reference the team could use when creating and reviewing documentation.
